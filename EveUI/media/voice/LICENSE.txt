EveUI ship-computer voice lines (*.ogg in this folder)

These clips are original recordings made for EveUI. They contain no audio from
EVE Online or World of Warcraft.

How they were made:
- Text-to-speech with Piper (https://github.com/rhasspy/piper, MIT licence),
  voice model en_GB "cori" (high) from https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices
  (repository licence: MIT). The voice was trained on public-domain LibriVox
  recordings (model card: "License: public domain").
- Post-processed with FFmpeg: silence trim, high-pass 180 Hz, low-pass 9.5 kHz,
  slight presence boost, light chorus, short echo, EBU R128 loudness
  normalisation (-16 LUFS), Ogg Vorbis, mono, 44.1 kHz.

The resulting clips are released under the same terms as EveUI itself and may
be redistributed with it.
